01 · Why Bangladesh

Why Indian students are choosing Bangladesh.

09 · Questions

Bangladesh MBBS, plainly answered.

Is MBBS from Bangladesh valid in India?
Yes. All colleges we work with are recognized by NMC, WHO and BMDC. After completing MBBS + internship in Bangladesh, you clear the FMGE/NExT exam to practice in India.
Is NEET mandatory for Bangladesh?
Yes. NEET qualification is mandatory to get the eligibility certificate from NMC. Bangladesh also has its own cutoff — typically 50%+ in PCB with minimum marks in Biology.
What is the total 5-year cost?
Between ₹22 Lakh (budget colleges) and ₹35 Lakh (top colleges like Dhaka National) — all inclusive. Significantly cheaper than Indian private colleges and most abroad destinations.
What is the Bangladesh equivalence certificate?
It's a mandatory document from the Bangladesh High Commission that confirms your 10th/12th marks are equivalent to Bangladesh's SSC/HSC. We handle this entire process for you.
Can I do internship in India?
Yes, under current NMC rules, Bangladesh graduates can complete their 1-year internship in India after clearing FMGE. This is one of the biggest advantages over Russia/Georgia.
Is Bangladesh safe for Indian students?
Yes. Over 8,000 Indian students are currently studying MBBS in Bangladesh. Colleges have separate Indian student hostels, wardens, and Indian mess facilities.
Do you offer education loans for Bangladesh?
Yes. We have tie-ups with SBI, HDFC Credila, Avanse and InCred for loans up to ₹40 Lakh with easy EMI options — specifically structured for Bangladesh MBBS fees.
