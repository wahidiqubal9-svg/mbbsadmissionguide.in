Philippines · 2026 Intake

MBBS in the Philippines — English and clinical exposure, longer route.

The Philippines is fully English-medium with US-style clinical training and heavy hospital exposure. It is also the longest route on this site, because the BS pre-med year sits before the MD. Read the duration trap first.

₹2.5–4.0 LTuition / year
5.5–6 yearsBS + MD
~10–12%FMGE pass (indicative)
NEET Qualified Required ·50% PCB Minimum ·NMC-Listed Only ·English Medium
01 · How the course is structured

BS pre-med, then MD. Both count.

🎓

Two degrees

Students first complete a BS in a science subject (about 1.5–2 years), then the four-year MD. Together they satisfy the NMC 54-month duration rule.

🏥

Clinical strength

Philippine medical schools are attached to busy teaching hospitals with high patient volume and English case discussion — the genuine advantage here.

🌪️

Typhoon season

June to November brings storms and class disruption in Manila and Cebu. Not a dealbreaker, but plan travel and exam dates around it.

📋

Verify listing per university

Only NMC-listed Philippine universities are valid, and the list changes. Check for your admission year and screenshot it.

02 · Money and reality

The full number, and the hard parts.

What does it really cost?+
₹2.5–4 L/year tuition plus ₹1.5–2 L/year living in Manila or Cebu, and the BS year adds cost before medicine begins. Total ₹36–50 L, similar to Georgia with a longer runway.
Is the BS year counted by NMC?+
The overall BS + MD duration clears 54 months, but confirm the specific university's structure is accepted and that its internship is in the same institute, in writing.
How is licensing in India handled?+
FMGE/NExT, same wall as everywhere. The strong English training helps the exam slightly but the pass rate is still around 10–12%. Do not enrol expecting the Philippines to be an exception.
Can I practise in the US from here?+
Some students target USMLE, and the US-style curriculum is closer to it than a Russian six-year course. It is still an independent, expensive track — ask the university for its match list.
03 · Fit

Choose the Philippines if, skip it if.

Choose it if:+
English-medium teaching and real hospital exposure matter more than a short course, and you may eventually target the US track.
Skip it if:+
The budget is under ₹35 L, or you want the shortest possible route back to India. Bangladesh is cheaper and closer with a better FMGE rate.
