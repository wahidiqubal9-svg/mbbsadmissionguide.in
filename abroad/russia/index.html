Russia · 2026 Intake

MBBS in Russia — cheap on paper, unforgiving at FMGE.

Russia is the largest supplier of Indian medical students abroad and also the largest source of FMGE failures. This page gives you both halves: real costs, and the pass-rate maths before you commit six years.

01 · The honest picture

What Russia actually is, in 2026.

💰

Cheapest legal route

Tuition from roughly ₹3 L/year at state universities. For families capped near ₹4 L/year total, Russia and Kazakhstan are the only NMC-compliant options that fit.

📉

Low FMGE pass rate

Historically 10–15% of Russian graduates clear the screening test on first attempt. Budget for one to two extra years of exam preparation after you return.

📋

Delisting risk is real

NMC has removed universities from its approved list in waves. A university approved when you join can lose listing mid-course — and your degree with it.

❄️

Language and climate

The first year is Russian language. Clinical rotations happen in Russian. Winters run to −20 °C in most cities. That is a genuine filter, not a marketing point.

02 · Money

Full six-year cost, not the brochure number.

Tuition

₹3.0–5.0 L/year depending on city and university tier. State universities are cheapest and also the most likely to drop off the NMC list.

Hostel

₹35,000–70,000/year in university dormitories. Private flats cost 2–3× that and are usually shared.

Living, visa, insurance

₹1.2–1.8 L/year for food, local transport, medical insurance, visa extension and the mandatory annual checks.

Realistic total

₹32–48 L across six years, before FMGE coaching (₹60k–1.5 L) and return travel. Anyone quoting ₹18 L all-in is quoting year one only.

03 · Eligibility

NMC rules that decide whether you can even go.

What does NMC require for MBBS abroad?+
Under the 2021 Graduate Medical Education Regulations: qualified NEET, minimum 50% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry and Biology (40% for reserved categories), English as a subject in 10+2, a course of at least 54 months plus 12 months internship at the same institute, and admission to an NMC-listed university only.
Is NEET mandatory for Russia?+
Yes. Qualifying NEET UG in the year of admission or the two years before is mandatory. Without it you cannot sit FMGE/NExT later, which makes the degree unusable in India.
How do I verify a university is listed?+
Check the NMC website's list of recognised foreign medical institutions for your admission year, not a consultancy PDF. Take a dated screenshot. If the university is missing, walk away regardless of the fee.
04 · After graduation

The part that decides your career.

How does licensing in India work?+
You must clear the screening test (FMGE, transitioning to NExT) to get registration. Until that is cleared the degree does not allow practice in India. Roughly 85–90% of Russian graduates do not clear on the first attempt.
When is Russia the wrong choice?+
If your family can reach ₹5 L+/year, Georgia or Uzbekistan usually beat Russia on English-medium teaching and climate. If you cannot self-study for a hard exam for a year after returning, the low fee will not save you.
Is the degree valid in other countries?+
Some graduates sit USMLE or PLAB. That is a separate, harder track — Russian clinical exposure and documentation support for ECFMG vary by university. Ask specific universities for their USMLE match history before choosing.
