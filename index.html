Frequently AskedQuick Answers

Is MBBS abroad valid in India?▾
Yes. All universities we partner with are recognized by NMC (National Medical Commission), WHO, and UNESCO. You just need to clear the FMGE/NExT screening test after graduation to practice in India.
Who will guide me — an agent or a doctor?▾
You'll be guided directly by our doctor founders — including a physician who studied MBBS in Russia and cleared FMGE. Every step, from your first call to post-admission support, is handled by our own in-house team.
Is NEET mandatory for MBBS abroad?▾
Yes, NEET qualification is mandatory for Indian students who want to practice in India after MBBS abroad, as per NMC regulations 2021.
What is the total cost of MBBS abroad?▾
Total 6-year cost ranges from ₹18 Lakh (Kyrgyzstan) to ₹45 Lakh (top Russian/Georgian universities), including tuition, hostel, food, and visa. Much cheaper than Indian private colleges.
Will you help after admission too?▾
Yes. We handle everything in-house — hostel, Indian mess, local support, parent updates, and FMGE/NExT mentorship throughout your course. We stay with you until you're registered in India.
Do you provide education loan assistance?▾
Yes. We have tie-ups with leading banks and NBFCs (SBI, HDFC Credila, Avanse, InCred) to help you get education loans up to ₹40 Lakh with easy EMI options.
