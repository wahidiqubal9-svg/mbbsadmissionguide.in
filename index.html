06 · Questions

Answers to the big questions.

How do I know if I should stay in India or go abroad?
If you can secure a government medical seat (typically NEET 650+), India is always the best choice. If NEET is 400–600 and private India is unaffordable (₹80L+), abroad — especially Bangladesh — becomes the smart ROI. Our doctor-founders evaluate every student honestly.
Is MBBS abroad valid in India?
Yes — as long as the university is NMC-recognized (all ours are) and listed with WHO/UNESCO. You must clear the FMGE/NExT screening exam after graduation. Bangladesh graduates clear it at 55%+ — that's our benchmark.
Is NEET mandatory for both India and abroad?
Yes. NEET is mandatory for Indian medical college admission, and per NMC regulations, NEET qualification is also mandatory for Indian students to practice in India after MBBS abroad.
Do you handle MCC & state counseling registration?
Yes. We handle complete MCC (AIQ) registration, state counseling registration, document upload, choice filling strategy, and round-by-round guidance — including Deemed University rounds.
What is NRI quota admission?
15% seats in private & deemed colleges are reserved for NRI-sponsored candidates. NEET qualification is required, fees range from ₹80L to ₹2Cr. We guide families through documentation, sponsor verification, and round strategy.
Do you assist with education loans?
Yes. We have tie-ups with SBI, HDFC Credila, Avanse and InCred for loans up to ₹40 Lakh with easy EMI options — structured for both Indian private college fees and abroad tuition.
