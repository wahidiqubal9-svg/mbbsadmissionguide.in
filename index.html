07 · Questions

Answers, plainly spoken.

Is MBBS abroad valid in India?
Yes. All universities we partner with are recognized by NMC, WHO and UNESCO. You simply clear the FMGE/NExT screening test after graduation to practice in India.
Who guides me — an agent or a doctor?
You're guided directly by our doctor founders — including Dr. Imtiyaz Ahmad, who studied MBBS in Russia and cleared FMGE. Every step is handled by our own in-house team.
Is NEET mandatory?
Yes. NEET qualification is mandatory for Indian students who wish to practice in India after MBBS abroad, as per NMC regulations.
What is the total cost?
The full 6-year cost ranges from ₹18 Lakh (Kyrgyzstan) to ₹45 Lakh (top Russian/Georgian universities), including tuition, hostel, food and visa — far below Indian private colleges.
Will you help after admission?
Yes. Hostel, Indian mess, local support, parent updates and FMGE/NExT mentorship — we stay with you until you're registered in India.
Do you assist with education loans?
Yes. Tie-ups with leading banks and NBFCs (SBI, HDFC Credila, Avanse, InCred) for loans up to ₹40 Lakh with easy EMI options.
