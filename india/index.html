MBBS in India · 2026 Intake

MBBS in India — the routes, the ranks, the real fees.

Four routes exist: All India Quota, state quota, deemed universities and NRI seats. Each has a different authority, different fees and a different bond. Read which one your rank actually reaches before you fill a single choice.

01 · Admission routes

Four doors. Only one fits your rank.

All India Quota (AIQ)

15% of government college seats, allotted by MCC on all-India rank. No domicile needed. Closing ranks are brutal — AIQ MBBS typically shuts around the 13,000–15,000 rank band.

State quota (85%)

State counselling, domicile and category rules apply. Closing ranks are far more lenient in high-seat states, which is why domicile is the single biggest lever in Indian admissions.

Deemed universities

No domicile, no state rank — but fees of ₹10–22 L/year and a separate deemed counselling round. A rank fallback for families with budget, not a bargain.

NRI quota

Sponsored seats needing an NRI sponsor's documents and relationship proof. Expensive, and rules on who qualifies tighten every year. Verify the specific college's NRI clause.

03 · Process

The sequence, without the myths.

Step 1 — registration+
Register on the MCC portal for AIQ/deemed and on your state portal for state quota, pay the fee, complete document upload. Registering in both is normal; a state allotment blocks AIQ allotment in later rounds.
Step 2 — choice filling and locking+
Fill choices in genuine preference order, then lock. Unlocked choices are not considered. Fill more choices than you think you need — allotment is strictly rank-then-choice order.
Step 3 — allotment and reporting+
If allotted, report to the college with originals and pay. Fail to report in the window and the seat lapses, with forfeiture rules attached.
Step 4 — upgrades+
Upgradation from round two can move you up, but the old seat is released. Understand the round-by-round rules before opting in; a careless upgrade can leave you with nothing.
04 · Documents

The originals you must hold.

Academic and identity+
NEET admit card and scorecard, 10th and 12th marksheets and certificates, Aadhaar, passport photos, and the category certificate in the prescribed central format where applicable.
Domicile and route proof+
Domicile certificate for state quota; NRI sponsor documents and relationship proof for NRI seats; disability certificate where relevant. Mismatched spellings across documents are the most common verification failure.
05 · Money and bonds

Fees are only half the cost.

Government colleges+
₹5,000–1 L/year in most states, plus hostel and mess. Many states attach a service bond of 1–5 years or a penalty of ₹10–50 L for skipping it.
Private and deemed+
₹6–22 L/year depending on state and course. Fee-regulation committees cap some states, not all. Confirm the recognised fee for your allotted college before locking choices.
West Bengal private colleges+
Fees are notified by the state and seats are allotted only through WB counselling. See the full list of 8 private colleges, with the counselling and domicile rules →
Bond reality+
Rural service bonds are real and enforced through bond agreements and original-document retention. Read the bond text, not the brochure summary.
