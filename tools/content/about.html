About

Run by doctors who sat the same exams.

Dr. Wahid Iqubal (MBBS, College of Medicine & Sagore Dutta Hospital, Kolkata) and Dr. Washim Akram (MBBS, Barind Medical College, Rajshahi — FMGE cleared) started this after watching agents sell seats they would never sit in.

Method

How we work.

One team, start to finish+
Calls, documents, university correspondence, visas and arrival support are handled in-house. No lead resale, no handover to a call centre.
Written numbers+
Fees, inclusions and refund rules come in writing before any payment. We reconfirm current figures with the university for your intake.
Our seat commitment+
We take on a student only when the NEET score, category and budget give a realistic seat in a college we list. If they do not, we say so on the first call and we do not take your deposit. If we take the case and cannot place you, your ₹10,000 caution deposit is refunded in full. That is a promise about our service — not a claim that anyone can guarantee a seat before counselling allotments.
What it costs+
Counselling, shortlisting, document checks and college correspondence are free. When you confirm you want to proceed we take a ₹10,000 caution deposit — refundable, and adjusted against your first payment to the college. Nothing else is charged by us.
NMC-first checks+
For every foreign university we verify the NMC recognised-institutions list for your admission year before recommending it, and screenshot the check.
