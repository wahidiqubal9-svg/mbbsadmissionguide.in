About

Run by doctors who sat the same exams.

Dr. Wahid Iqubal (MBBS, College of Medicine & Sagore Dutta Hospital, Kolkata) and Dr. Washim Akram (MBBS, Barind Medical College, Rajshahi — FMGE cleared) started this after watching agents sell seats they would never sit in.

Method

How we work.

One team, start to finish+
Calls, documents, university correspondence, visas and arrival support are handled in-house. No lead resale, no handover to a call centre.
Written numbers+
Fees, inclusions and refund rules come in writing before any payment. We reconfirm current figures with the university for your intake.
No guaranteed seats+
We quote last year's closing ranks and tell you the score bands where you do not stand a chance. Counselling is guidance, not a promise.
NMC-first checks+
For every foreign university we verify the NMC recognised-institutions list for your admission year before recommending it, and screenshot the check.
Disclosure

How we earn.

Fees+
Our counselling is free for students. We are paid by partner universities when an admission completes, and the fee you pay the university is the same either way. We say upfront when a country or college pays us nothing.
India counselling+
Choice-filling guidance for MCC and state rounds is a paid service, priced before we begin.
