Verdict

Who should pick which.

Pick Bangladesh if:+
You want the best FMGE odds within a mid budget and family can visit easily. It is the better India-career choice and the cheaper one.
Pick Georgia if:+
Budget is comfortable past ₹40 L, you specifically value English-medium teaching and European living standards, and you may later look beyond India for specialisation.
The trap to avoid:+
Choosing Georgia on "EU" marketing alone. Georgia is not an EU member and the degree does not auto-license you in the EU. If India practice is the goal, Bangladesh's pass rate is the more valuable asset.
