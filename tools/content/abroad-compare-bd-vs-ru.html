Verdict

Who should pick which.

Pick Bangladesh if:+
You can fund ₹32 L+ and want the best available odds of actually practising in India. The FMGE gap is worth more than the price gap over a career.
Pick Russia if:+
The budget is strictly under ₹3 L/year, you will accept a language year, and you verify an NMC-listed university. Russia's cheap seats still exist; they are just less forgiving.
What about Bangladesh's quota problem?+
Bangladesh restricts foreign seats in some years and raises fees. Confirm seat availability and the current government fee for your intake before paying any booking amount.
