02 · The trade-off

Cheapest is not the same as cheapest to finish.

Why is Bangladesh mid-priced but still often the better deal?+
FMGE pass rates of roughly 25–34% versus 10–15% elsewhere mean fewer retakes. Each failed FMGE attempt costs a year of coaching (₹1–1.5 L) and a year of lost salary. On a five-year horizon Bangladesh frequently ends up cheaper net.
Do cheaper countries have weaker universities?+
Not automatically, but the cheapest universities are the ones most exposed to NMC delisting. Cross-check every university against the NMC list for your batch regardless of the country's ranking here.
Can I work part-time to fund it?+
Most countries permit limited part-time work, but the first two years are too heavy to rely on it. Treat earnings as pocket money, not tuition.
