Caveats

Four reasons not to trust this table blindly.

Cohort size+
A country with 40 candidates can swing 10 percentage points on five results. Rates for tiny cohorts are noise.
University variation+
Within Bangladesh or the Philippines the best college can run triple the rate of a weak one. Ask for the university's own figures, not the country's.
Who sits the exam+
Only graduates who choose to appear are counted. Weak students skipping attempts flatter the rate; strong students sitting early depress it.
Coaching effect+
Most passes follow paid coaching in India. Country and university set your baseline; your coaching and discipline decide the outcome. Plan and budget for it from day one.
