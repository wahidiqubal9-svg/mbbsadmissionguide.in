01 · What you are buying

Convenience, not a shortcut.

🗣️

No language year

Teaching, exams and hospital interaction are in English, and Georgian is optional. That removes the single biggest dropout cause in Russia and Central Asia.

🎓

Bologna-format MD

ECTS-structured degree from a European-integration system. Useful for a future EU specialisation, but it does not by itself license you in the EU.

💸

Mid-budget tier

₹3.8–6 L/year tuition plus ₹1.5–2 L/year living. Total ₹38–52 L. Families below ₹4.5 L/year should look at Uzbekistan instead.

📉

Same FMGE wall

English medium does not raise pass rates much — 10–14% clear on the first attempt. The gap is exam discipline, not comprehension.

02 · Verify these before paying

Four checks that protect six years of your life.

Is the university on the NMC list for my batch?+
Check the NMC list of recognised foreign medical institutions for your admission year and screenshot it with the date visible. Names change; a consultancy's screenshot from three years ago proves nothing.
Is the course 54 months plus 12 months internship?+
NMC requires the full course and internship in the same institute. Confirm the contract states six years including internship, and that internship is in Georgia — not a "return to India" arrangement.
What is in the fee and what is extra?+
Hostel, mess, insurance, visa extension, lab and exam fees are commonly excluded. Ask for a written year-by-year schedule with every line item, and the refund rule if the visa is refused.
What are the intakes and the visa timeline?+
Main intake is September, with a smaller February window at some universities. Study visa processing needs an invitation letter and takes 3–6 weeks. Start 4 months before the intake, and never fly on a tourist visa.
03 · Reality check

When Georgia is worth the premium — and when it is not.

Worth it if:+
You can fund ₹40 L+ without loans, you want English-medium teaching with no language year, and an EU-integration degree matters for your later plans. Tbilisi has direct flights, an Indian student community and Indian restaurants.
Not worth it if:+
Your budget is under ₹4 L/year (go Uzbekistan or Bangladesh), or you are choosing Georgia purely because it sounds European. Patients in Georgian hospitals speak Georgian; clinical years still involve translation.
How does licensing work afterwards?+
FMGE/NExT for India, same as every other country. For the UK, Georgia is not on the GMC's acceptable-qualification route without additional steps — verify before assuming a PLAB path.
