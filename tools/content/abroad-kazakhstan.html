Kazakhstan · 2026 Intake

MBBS in Kazakhstan — budget medicine, Central Asian winters.

Kazakhstan sits between Russia and Uzbekistan on both price and quality. It is workable if the university passes the NMC check and you can handle a language year in Russian or Kazakh.

01 · The basics

What you need to know before anything else.

💸

Cost

₹2.5–4 L/year tuition, ₹35–60k/year hostel, ₹1–1.4 L/year living. Total ₹26–38 L — about ₹6–10 L less than Georgia.

🗣️

Language

One year of Russian or Kazakh is standard before medicine starts. Clinical years run in the local language, so this is not optional.

📋

NMC list

Several Kazakh universities were dropped from the NMC list in past cycles. Verify yours against the list for your admission year and keep the screenshot.

🌡️

Climate

−15 to −25 °C winters in Almaty, Karaganda and Semey. Indian mess is available in the larger student cities but not everywhere.

02 · Due diligence

Ask these four things in writing.

Is the six years inclusive of internship?+
NMC requires a 54-month course plus 12-month internship at the same institute. Get the contract to state the total duration and that internship is in Kazakhstan.
What exactly is in the fee?+
Demand a year-by-year written schedule marking hostel, mess, insurance, visa and registration separately, plus the refund rule for visa refusal and the rule if the university is delisted mid-course.
Who teaches, and in what language?+
Ask for the language of instruction for years 1–6 and whether Indian faculty are involved. Universities advertising "English medium" often switch to Russian after year two.
What is the FMGE history of this university?+
Not the country average — the university's own. Ask how many of its Indian graduates cleared. Most cannot answer precisely; that itself is information.
03 · Fit

Choose Kazakhstan if, skip it if.

Choose it if:+
Budget is ₹3–4 L/year and fixed, you are comfortable learning a new language, and a verified NMC-listed university is available at that price.
Skip it if:+
You need English-medium teaching (go Georgia), the budget stretches to ₹5 L/year (Philippines or Bangladesh), or the only affordable university is not on the NMC list for your batch.
