01 · Why families pick it

Straight answers on the cheapest option.

💰

Lowest total

₹22–34 L over six years including hostel and living. It is the only option under ₹2.5 L/year tuition that is still NMC-compliant.

🛂

Simpler visa

Student visa processing is comparatively quick and rarely refused. That short timeline is also why some families choose before checking the university properly.

🏥

Growing Indian cohort

Tashkent, Samarkand and Bukhara host a rising number of Indian students. Indian mess and communities exist in the big cities, less so elsewhere.

⚠️

Newest universities

Many private medical institutes are under ten years old with no FMGE track record. New does not mean bad, but it means unproven — and delistable.

02 · Verification

The five checks before any payment.

NMC listing for my batch+
Screenshot the NMC recognised-institutions list with the date, for your admission year. If it is not there, stop — no fee discount compensates for an unusable degree.
54 months + 12 months internship+
Must be in the contract and in the same institute. Watch for "internship in India" claims — under NMC rules it belongs abroad.
Refund and delisting clauses+
Written policy for visa refusal, and what happens if the university loses listing while you study. Vague answers are a red flag.
Language and faculty+
Year one is language prep. Confirm the language of instruction for clinical years and how many permanent faculty teach in English.
Real hostel and mess costs+
Get photos and the actual monthly rate, not "hostel included". Food is almost always extra and costs ₹6–10k/month.
03 · Fit

Choose Uzbekistan if, skip it if.

Choose it if:+
The budget genuinely stops near ₹3 L/year and a verified NMC-listed university fits. You are prepared to self-study hard for FMGE — the pass rate here is unforgiving.
Skip it if:+
You can reach ₹30–35 L comfortably (Bangladesh gives a far better pass rate), or you need English-medium teaching, or only unlisted universities fit your budget.
