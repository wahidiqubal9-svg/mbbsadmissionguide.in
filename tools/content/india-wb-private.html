02 · How allotment works

One counselling system, no back doors.

Who conducts counselling+
State-quota seats in West Bengal medical colleges are allotted through the state's NEET UG counselling. All India Quota seats in government colleges go through MCC separately. Nobody can "hold" a private college seat for you outside counselling.
Domicile+
West Bengal state-quota seats require WB domicile, with conditions around where you studied. A Kolkata address alone is not enough — the certificate and the eligibility rule are checked at verification.
Rounds and reporting+
Allotment happens in rounds. If you are allotted a seat you must report with originals and pay within the window, or the seat lapses. Upgradation rules apply from the later rounds — read them before opting in.
The bond question+
Bond and service conditions in West Bengal differ between government and private colleges and change with state notifications. Confirm the bond attached to your specific allotted seat before you report — a wrong assumption here costs lakhs.
03 · Fit

Is a West Bengal private college right for you?

It makes sense if+
You are NEET qualified, have WB domicile, and want to stay in the state with clinical exposure in Bengali-speaking hospitals. It also keeps the family close during five and a half years, which matters more than people admit.
Compare it against+
Domicile-free options cost more but need no WB certificate; government colleges in the state cost a fraction but need a far better rank; Bangladesh is cheaper than most WB private colleges and a two-hour flight away — but FMGE applies.
