Finance

Education loans for MBBS abroad — before you sign anything.

Banks lend for NMC-listed universities, and mostly against collateral. Understand the moratorium, the interest during study, and what happens if you fail FMGE.

The basics

How the market works.

Secured vs unsecured+
Above roughly ₹7.5 L banks want collateral — property, FD or LIC. Unsecured loans exist only for premier Indian institutes. For MBBS abroad, assume collateral will be required.
Co-applicant+
A parent or guardian must co-sign. Their income, CIBIL score and ITRs decide the sanction. A score below 700 usually means rejection regardless of your NEET rank.
Moratorium+
Repayment starts after the course plus a grace period, but interest accrues from day one. A ₹35 L loan at 11% becomes ₹50 L+ by the time you finish — plan for monthly interest servicing if you can.
NMC listing matters+
Banks check the NMC list. Admission to an unlisted university can kill the sanction mid-process. Get the loan approved before paying a booking amount.
If FMGE is failed+
EMI continues regardless. Some lenders allow a short extension during repeat attempts — ask explicitly and get it in writing.
Documents

What you will be asked for.

Student side+
Admission letter, NEET scorecard, 10th/12th marksheets, Aadhaar, PAN, passport and photographs.
Co-applicant side+
Income proof (ITR/salary slips), bank statements, collateral papers, CIBIL consent and KYC. Any mismatch between the admission letter and the loan application delays sanction by weeks.
