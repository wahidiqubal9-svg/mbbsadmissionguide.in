Upcoming Exam

NExT — the licensing exam that replaces FMGE.

The National Exit Test is designed to unify licensing for Indian and foreign graduates. It has been announced, revised and delayed repeatedly. Here is the current state without alarmism.

Status

What is confirmed and what is not.

Is NExT running yet?+
Not fully. NMC has published regulations for NExT but the exam has not been operationalised for all graduates. Until then FMGE remains the screening test for foreign graduates — check NMC and NBEMS notifications before relying on any date.
What will it change+
In principle, one licensing exam for everyone, with a clinical/practical component in addition to theory. It is expected to be harder and more practical than FMGE, not easier.
Does it affect my eligibility rules+
No. NEET qualification, the 50% PCB requirement, NMC-listed university and the 54-month-plus-internship rule are separate and unaffected.
What should a current student do+
Prepare the same way you would for FMGE — Indian PG material, clinical subjects first, a fixed revision schedule. NExT's clinical emphasis makes real ward exposure more valuable, not less.
