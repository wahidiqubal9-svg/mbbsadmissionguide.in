Funding

Scholarships for MBBS abroad — the realistic list.

Full scholarships for undergraduate medicine abroad are rare. Partial tuition waivers and merit discounts are common. Here is what exists, and what consultancies overstate.

Truth first

Set expectations before applying.

Full funding?+
Rare for MBBS abroad. Government schemes mostly fund Indian colleges. A handful of foreign universities offer partial waivers to very high NEET scorers — usually 10–30% of tuition in year one only.
Merit discounts+
Some universities discount for NEET scores above roughly 500. Ask in writing, get it into the admission letter, and confirm whether it renews each year or applies to year one only.
Indian schemes+
State-level and central schemes rarely cover MBBS abroad. A few states reimburse part of the fee for reserved-category students — check your state's medical education department, not a consultancy claim.
Exchange-rate effects+
A 10% rouble or tenge move can wipe out a 10% discount. Budget a buffer of at least ₹2 L per year.
