What you pay us+
Counselling is free. A ₹10,000 caution deposit is payable when you confirm you want us to proceed. It is refunded in full if we cannot secure a seat, refunded on request if you withdraw before counselling work begins, and adjusted against your first payment to the college if you are admitted. It is never a payment for a seat — seats are allotted by the relevant counselling authority or university.
Third-party doctors+
Admissions for a country are handled by the partner doctor who practises in that system. They act as an independent professional, and their conduct, advice and any contract with you are their own. We coordinate and remain accountable for the service you receive from us.
Guidance, not guarantee+
We provide counselling and information. A seat, a rank or a visa is never guaranteed. Admission depends on NEET results, counselling rules, university decisions and government policy.
Fees are indicative+
Fees, cutoffs and pass rates shown are indicative and change with exchange rates and annual revisions. The university's current written offer prevails.
NMC listing+
We verify the NMC list at the time of recommendation, but listing status can change. Students should re-verify before payment. See the NMC website for the authoritative list.
Licensing+
Studying abroad does not license you in India. FMGE/NExT must be cleared. Pass-rate figures are indicative and not a prediction of your result.
Third parties+
Universities, hostels, banks and visa authorities are independent. Their rules, delays and refusals are outside our control.
Liability+
Our liability for any claim is limited to the counselling fee paid to us, where applicable. We are not liable for consequential losses such as lost academic years.
